      **Applied Scholastics Online Academy** (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is an
      educational institution committed to protecting the privacy of our
      students, parents, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we
      collect, use, and protect personal information through our website and
      mobile applications.


      ## Information We Collect


      We may collect personal information such as names, contact details,
      academic information, and usage data necessary to provide educational
      services and maintain student records.


      ## How We Use Information


      We use collected information solely for educational and administrative
      purposes, including providing and improving our online learning services,
      managing student accounts and enrollment, communicating with students and
      parents, and complying with legal and regulatory requirements.


      ## Data Sharing


      We do **not** sell or share personal information with third parties except
      as necessary in the normal course of business — for example, with service
      providers who assist us in delivering our educational programs or
      complying with legal obligations. All such partners are required to
      protect your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.


      ## Data Retention and Removal


      We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill educational,
      administrative, or legal requirements. Requests for data removal (except
      for records required by law) may be made by emailing
      [accounts@apsonlineacademy.com](mailto\:accounts@apsonlineacademy.com).


      ## Security


      We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to
      safeguard personal information against loss, misuse, or unauthorized
      access.


      ## Updates to This Policy


      We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
      posted on our website and app with an updated effective date.


      **Contact Us:** If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy,
      please contact us at
      [accounts@apsonlineacademy.com](mailto\:accounts@apsonlineacademy.com).
