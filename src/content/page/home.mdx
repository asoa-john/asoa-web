  - brow: Frequently Asked Questions
    heading: Applied Scholastics Online Academy
    items:
      - question: What is Applied Scholastics Online Academy?
        answer: >
          We’re an accredited homeschool oversight program that supports parents
          while they teach their children at home. Parents stay in charge — we
          provide assessments, customized  educational programs at each level,
          structure, records, and guidance.
      - question: Are you accredited?
        answer: >
          Yes. Our accreditation means your child’s records, transcripts, and
          diploma are recognized and respected by schools and colleges. They are
          kept on-hand to be available as needed by your family.
      - question: How is this different from homeschooling on my own?
        answer: >
          You still run the show, but we make the journey smoother!  We provide
          a program designed for your child, and your child alone and offer the
          materials to go with it.  You may also substitute materials that you
          prefer.  We keep official records and are a friendly team cheering you
          on with any advice that you may need!
      - question: Who can enroll?
        answer: >
          Any K–12 student who is homeschooled and needs accredited oversight,
          academic structure, official records and a supportive team in their
          corner! For little children ready and reaching to read, we can enroll
          them as young as 4 years old for our “reading readiness program.
      - question: Can we enroll mid-year?
        answer: >
          Yes, we offer year-round enrollment. Families are welcome to start
          whenever they’re ready.
      - question: Must we use a specific curriculum?
        answer: >
          Not at all. We make suggestions but you decide.  While states and
          countries require that students study subjects, such as reading,
          writing and math, there are hundreds of ways to fulfill such
          requirements. We help families explore options and choose curriculum
          that fits their child’s learning style, pace, and interests.
      - question: How flexible is the program?
        answer: >
          Very flexible. You set the daily schedule and learning pace. We
          provide encouragement, accountability, and support! Families take
          learning with them when they travel or move to another state (or
          country that permits homeschool). We offer dual enrollment so your
          child’s education can include the arts, church classes, sports,
          scouts, online learning, field trips, travel, or other learning
          opportunities your child loves. We’re here to back you up!
      - question: What records do I need to submit?
        answer: >
          We require a monthly progress/attendance report, yearly assessments
          (which we provide for you or your tutor to deliver) and sample student
          work.
